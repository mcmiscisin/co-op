How to play
  1. Enter whole-number coefficients (use 1 for “no change”).
  2. Press Enter or click Check.
  3. Hint shows atom counts LHS vs RHS so you can nudge toward balance.
  4. Show answer reveals one valid balanced set.
Why this matters

Balancing enforces the Law of Conservation of Mass—atoms aren’t created or destroyed. You’re matching the number of each atom on both sides.

Great practice for stoichiometry and for spotting polyatomic groups that carry through reactions.

The beam tilts based on total atoms (a simple “mass” proxy). When counts match, it levels.
